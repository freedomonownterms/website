AI image generators turn a text description into a picture in seconds. They're useful for social posts, blog headers, presentations, and creative projects, even if you can't draw.

## Which tool should you start with?

- **Adobe Firefly:** a good choice for business use, and built into Photoshop.
- **Midjourney:** beautiful, artistic results. Paid only.
- **Ideogram:** the best choice when you need readable text in the image, such as posters or thumbnails.
- **ChatGPT and Gemini:** convenient if you already use them, since you can create images right in the chat.

## How to write a good prompt

A strong prompt describes four things:

1. **Subject:** what's in the image ("a golden retriever wearing headphones")
2. **Setting:** where it is ("in a cozy home studio")
3. **Style:** how it looks ("soft watercolor illustration" or "realistic photo")
4. **Mood and lighting:** "warm morning light, calm and cheerful"

Put together:

> A golden retriever wearing headphones in a cozy home studio, soft watercolor illustration, warm morning light, calm and cheerful.

## Tips for better results

- **Iterate.** Your first result is rarely the best. Change one detail at a time.
- **Name the format.** Add "wide banner," "square," or "vertical phone wallpaper."
- **Be specific about style.** "Flat vector illustration" gives a very different result from "cinematic photo."

## Use images responsibly

- Don't create images of real people in misleading situations.
- Check each tool's license before using images commercially.
- Say when an image is AI-generated if your audience would care.

Start with something fun, experiment freely, and you'll quickly get a feel for what each tool does best.
