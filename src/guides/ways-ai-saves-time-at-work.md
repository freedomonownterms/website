You don't need to be technical to get real value from AI. These five everyday tasks are where most people see the biggest time savings.

## 1. Summarizing long documents

Paste a long report, contract, or email thread into an assistant like **Claude** or **ChatGPT** and ask for the key points, decisions, and deadlines. For a whole folder of documents, try **NotebookLM**.

## 2. Meeting notes and action items

Tools like **Otter.ai** record and transcribe meetings, then produce a summary and a list of who agreed to do what. No more frantic note-taking.

## 3. First drafts of anything

Proposals, blog posts, job descriptions, social posts: ask AI for a first draft and spend your time improving it instead of staring at a blank page.

## 4. Research with sources

**Perplexity** searches the web and shows where each answer came from, so you can check the facts quickly. It's great for comparing products or getting up to speed on a new topic.

## 5. Spreadsheets and formulas

Describe what you want in plain English, for example "sum column C where column A is 'Paid'", and **Copilot** or **Gemini** will write the formula and explain it.

## Start small

Pick **one** task from this list and use AI for it every day this week. Once it becomes a habit, add another. Small, consistent wins add up to hours every week.
